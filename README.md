### Hi, I'm Tyler 👋

I'm a UX product designer. I design it, then I build it.

I want to build experiences that feel exciting, novel and fun: sites and interfaces that are genuinely pleasing to look at, and to use. Building with AI lets me take an idea past a mockup and into something people can actually try. That's how everything here got made.
